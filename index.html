The Simpsons is a long‑running American animated sitcom created by Matt Groening, known for its satirical take on American life through the misadventures of the Simpson family — Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie. It first aired on December 17, 1989 and has become the longest‑running scripted primetime TV series in history, with 37 seasons and over 800 episodes.
